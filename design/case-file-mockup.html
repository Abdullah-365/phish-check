Case A7F3-21 · "Action required: your account has been limited"Inspected 25 Oct 2026, 12:41 · in your browser

This email pretends to be PayPal. It isn't.

Don't click its link or reply. If you have a PayPal account, open the app or type paypal.com yourself to check it.

Exhibit A · What you saw

P
PayPal Service1to me · Fri 23 Oct, 09:41

Action required: your account has been limited

Dear Customer,

We noticed unusual activity on your account and have temporarily limited it. To restore full access, confirm your details within 24 hours3:

https://www.paypal.com/signin2

If you do not confirm, your account will be permanently suspended.

Inspector's notes · What's really there

1 The sender isn't PayPal

You see
PayPal Service
Really
service@paypa1-security.test

The name is free to type. The address uses a 1 in place of an l, and PayPal's own mail servers didn't send it.

2 The link goes somewhere else

You see
https://www.paypal.com/signin
Really
http://paypa1-security.test/login/verify?u=you@…

The link text is just a label. Clicking it opens a lookalike site, and your email address is already filled into the link.

3 It rushes you

"Within 24 hours" and "permanently suspended" are pressure tactics. Real companies don't threaten deadlines by email.

Sender checks

  • Domain policy (DMARC)fail
  • Allowed server (SPF)softfail
  • Signature (DKIM)none
  • Replies go toother domain

The route it took

  1. vps-77.hosting.test198.51.100.77 · rented server
  2. mx.inbox.exampleyour mail provider · 02:41:18

Attachments

None. There's also a hidden 1×1 tracking image that tells the sender when you open the email.

What to do now

  1. Don't click the link or download anything from this email.
  2. Report it. In Gmail: ⋮ → Report phishing.
  3. Already clicked and signed in? Change your PayPal password now and turn on two-step login.
